# A Folder of Slide Screenshots Becomes One Lean PDF

## Why Care?

When a deck sits behind a viewer that can't be downloaded, someone screenshots
it page by page. Those screenshots have to become one PDF before a memo run or
a deal slide can use them. The settings that keep that PDF small and still
legible were worked out once, in memopop's `slim_deck.py`, and then lived only
in a script and a plan. Now any session can find them by name.

## What changed

- **`convert-images-to-pdf`**, with `scripts/images-to-pdf.py` (uv inline
  dependencies, no project needed).
- **Settled defaults:** long side 1600 px, JPEG q85 `optimize=True`, pages
  assembled with PyMuPDF at the image's own size. These were measured at
  28.0 MB to 1.4 MB on a retina-screenshot deck.
- **Capture-time order.** macOS screenshot names carry the time, and capture
  order is page order. A filename sort puts 10 AM before 9 AM and PM before AM
  across noon. `--dry-run` prints the order before anything is written.
- **Safe by default.** It refuses to overwrite and never deletes an input. The
  viewer-chrome crop applies only when every page agrees on the same box.
