# Shares Down to the Edge

## Why Care?

We share mostly through iMessage and WhatsApp, often one-to-one. A link that
unfurls as the site logo, or as the whole page when someone meant one section,
wastes that share. Two sites now get it right, and this skill lets the next
site copy them instead of rediscovering it.

## What's New?

- **One recipe for share cards**: a card per page and per h2/h3, rendered tall
  (`og:image`) and wide (`twitter:image`) at build time.
- **Section share pages** under `/s/`, because chat apps ignore `#fragments`.
- **Content versus site shares**: content gets the metadata card; home, about
  and index pages can use an Ideogram illustration set in all four shapes.
- **A file map** of what to copy from `cathode-site` and `fullstack-vc`, the
  rules that bit us, and a verification checklist.
- **The edge layer** that would hand LinkedIn, Bluesky and Discord the wide
  card is written up as planned, not built.
