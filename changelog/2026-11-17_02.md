# Build-Time Share Cards Become the Default OG Technique

## Why Care?

`generate-consistent-og-images` offered two ways to make a share image: an
Ideogram illustration or a screenshot of the site's hero. Both make one image
by hand, so in practice every page on a site shared the same card. Two sites
shipped a third way today, a card per page drawn from the page's own content at
build time. A shared link to one Cathode write-up showed up in iMessage as a
clean tall card with that write-up's photo and title. That's now the default.

## What's New?

- **Technique C: per-page cards rendered at build time.** satori and sharp
  draw a tall 1080×1440 `og:image` and a wide 1200×630 `twitter:image` for every
  page, event, article and section, from a static endpoint. No runtime and no
  host-specific API.
- **It's the default for any site with more than a few pages.** Ideogram
  (Technique A) and hero rasterization (Technique B) now make the site card,
  wired the same way: tall as `og:image`, wide as `twitter:image`.
- The skill's description names all three, so it loads for "per-page share
  images" requests too.

## Under the Hood

The pattern, design rules and the bugs we hit are in the astro-knots blueprint
`Share-Every-Page-and-Section-With-Its-Own-Image`. Reference implementations:
`astro-knots/sites/cathode-site` (dark brand, LFM) and
`astro-knots/sites/edviro-blog` (light brand, Astro MDX).
