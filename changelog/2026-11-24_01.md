# Memos Become Decile Hub Deal Memo Forms

Decile Hub's fund admin team wants a deal memo on every deal, and the Hub records it as a
form rather than a file: nine rating categories scored 1, 2, 4 or 5, plus 29 questions in
four groups. `refactor-memo-to-form-requirements` turns the memo we already wrote into
that form.

It picks the memo version that best matches the investment date. Early portfolio
companies were backfilled after the fact, so the skill takes the earliest version after
the investment, keeps later facts out, and prefers answers already on the Hub that were
written at the time. It drafts what the memo supports, leaves the partner's attestations
(conflicts, compensation, value-add) to the partner, and has the partner confirm the
ratings before they're written under their name.

The reference, `references/decile-hub-deal-memo-form.md`, was read live from the CogScAI
SPV memo and a Fund I memo. Both use the same template, so it is Humain Ventures' standard
deal memo, not an SPV-only form.
