# The Splash Catalog Finds Vendored Skills Nested Three Levels Down

## Why Care?

A vendored skill that loads in Claude Code but never appears in the catalog is invisible to anyone browsing what we use. Four were: `floorplan`, `agent-reach`, `chroma-local` and `chroma-cloud`.

## What changed

The loader looked for `SKILL.md` one and two folders down. Plugin-style repos keep their skills at `<repo>/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, and Agent Reach at `agent-reach/agent_reach/skill/SKILL.md`, three down. The loader now also matches those two layouts exactly. A blanket depth-three match would have listed Chroma's skills twice, since that repo keeps source copies under `src/`.
