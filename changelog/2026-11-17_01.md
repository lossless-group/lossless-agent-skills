## Why Care?

A new site went live with no share image at all, and nothing in the skills said
it should have one. Long-form pages had a quieter gap: sharing `page#heading`
always unfurls as the whole page, because chat apps ignore the fragment.

## What's New?

- **`open-graph-share-seo-geo`:** a new rule, "Share every page and every section
  with its own image": build-time cards (satori, resvg, sharp), tall og:image and
  wide twitter:image, `/s/` share pages for sections, heading ids from LFM, and
  the satori gotchas (WOFF not WOFF2, explicit text widths).
- **`astro-knots`:** share images are now standard equipment, like search.
- **The Open Graph playbook** points to the new blueprint,
  `astro-knots/context-v/blueprints/Share-Every-Page-and-Section-With-Its-Own-Image.md`,
  with `sites/cathode-site` as the reference implementation.
