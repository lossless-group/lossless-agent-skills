## What changed

- `graphify/` now holds what `graphify install` writes for 0.9.113: `SKILL.md`, six updated files in `references/`, and `.graphify_version`.
- `VENDORED.md` records the new version and refresh date.
- Outside this repo, on the same day: the `lossless-group/graphify` fork was synced with upstream (`v8` branch), the `graphifyy` CLI was upgraded with `uv tool upgrade`, and the skill was reinstalled into `~/.claude/skills` and `~/.agents/skills`.

## Why

The vendored copy had sat at 0.9.32 since 2026-10-05, and it still named the upstream by its old address. The skill drives the CLI, so the two have to move together.
