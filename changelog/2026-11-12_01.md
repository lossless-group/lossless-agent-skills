## Why Care?

The first real board (Mandatory Ventures) showed two gaps. The 1960s annual
report covers looked right, but covers alone say nothing about layout, and
layout is what the site borrows. And the person kept pointing at live websites
("like gem.com"), which had no place on a board built only for image pins.
Meanwhile `astro-knots` never mentioned the narrative or the board, so a new
site could skip straight to components.

## What's New?

- **`astro-knots`** names the design run-up for any site with a public face:
  narrative (`write-site-narrative`), then mood board (`build-a-moodboard`),
  then brand kit and `DESIGN.md`. The board stays in `design/moodboard/` and
  never deploys. Mandatory Ventures is the reference implementation.
- **Insides, not just covers.** For print worlds, `build-a-moodboard` now pulls
  interior pages and spreads, about as many as covers: multi-page scans from
  the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons PDFs, a contact sheet to pick
  from, and ImageMagick to join facing pages into spreads. It warns that page
  indices are often off by one, so every join gets looked at.
- **Websites as inspiration cards.** An optional `sites` array renders link
  cards in the lossless-toolkit ToolCard shape: the 1200×630 OG image (or a
  hero screenshot saved locally when there is none), title, domain, summary,
  tags, and a `steal` line. The template ships the styles and renderer.
- **A decisions log.** Reactions go in `design/moodboard/decisions.md`, one
  dated entry per round, in the person's words.
