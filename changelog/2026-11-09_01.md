## Why Care?

Coding agents are faster and better at new features, pages and UI when they
aren't thinking about the existing component library. So agents now make a
gametime decision to use it or not, and when the emphasis is creative they
break free of it, then refactor back into it. That second half is the
discipline most developers forget, and it slows the team down later.

## What's New?

- **`spike-then-refactor-into-components`.** The decision rule, the
  `@spike <name>` marker, and the refactor procedure: lock behavior with a
  snapshot test, reuse or extend components before creating new ones, refactor
  back onto the theme's tokens in all three modes, catalog everything, close
  the spike. Also how to hand the refactor to its own agent.
- **`maintain-design-system-page`.** The executable version of the astro-knots
  blueprint `Maintain-Design-System-and-Brandkit-Motions`: the two pages'
  scopes, the entry each component needs, the same-change rule, the theme as
  part of the design system, and how to catch up a catalog that has drifted.
- Both are checked by `knot-check components` in
  [astro-knots-mcp](https://github.com/lossless-group/astro-knots-mcp):
  uncatalogued components, open spikes, oversized pages, and raw colors or
  fonts outside the theme.
