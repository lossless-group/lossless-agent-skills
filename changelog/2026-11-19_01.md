## What changed

`git-conventions` gains a rule: after `git push origin <a>:<b>` (or `HEAD:<b>`), update local `<b>` in the same step, preferably with `git fetch origin <b>:<b>`, which refuses anything but a fast-forward.

## Why

In astro-knots, three sessions promoted `development` and pushed submodule bumps straight to `origin/master`. Local `master` never moved, and another session committed on it. The result was a branch that looked like someone on another machine had pushed, when every commit came from this laptop.
