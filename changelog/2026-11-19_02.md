## What changed

- **`THIRD-PARTY.md`** picks the shape by what the upstream repo is:
  - a ready skill folder we load becomes a submodule (forked if we'll patch it);
  - a skill generated by an installer becomes a vendored copy with `VENDORED.md`;
  - a pack we only mine goes under `context-v/skill-packs/` and is never linked;
  - study material goes in `ai-labs/studies/`.
- It also says our own skills name a third-party dependency instead of copying it, and that every one gets a row in this README and in the root monorepo README.
- The README now lists `humanizer` and `talk-normal`, which were submodules with no row.
- `context-vigilance` adds `context-v/skill-packs/` to its experimental folders.

## Why

Third-party skills had arrived four different ways (submodules, forks, a vendored copy, a pack) with the reasons scattered across commits and one `VENDORED.md`. Adding `seo-skills`, `humanizer` and the graphify question on the same day made the missing rule obvious.
