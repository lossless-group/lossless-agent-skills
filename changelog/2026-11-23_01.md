# Finished Work Ships Without Asking

## Why Care?

The operator kept having to come back and say "merge it, sync the tiers, push." The skills taught
agents to hold: leave parent pointers dirty until wrap-up, and wait for a go-ahead before
committing a client-site. Once the tests pass and the operator has seen the UI work, that wait
does nothing but cost time.

## What changed

- **git-conventions:** the parent pointer is bumped as the last step of shipping, with the
  parent's tiers fast-forwarded too. Several child commits still share one bump.
- **pseudomonorepos:** the same rhythm in its table: bump when the work ships, without asking.
- **remake-deck:** commit and push the client-site once the variants play in the shell.
