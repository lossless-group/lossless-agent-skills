## What changed

- New skill `slim-file-sizes/`. Its standard is legibility, not a byte budget: text stays selectable and vector, and everything reads on a retina laptop at 100%. Every file goes through, not only the large ones.
- Six scripts, each taking one file and never deleting anything:
  - `pdf-compress.sh`: Ghostscript with images to 200 dpi. Refuses if text is lost or the file grows.
  - `deck-to-pdf.sh`: Keynote or PowerPoint export, listing embedded video first.
  - `video-720p.sh`, `image-to-webp.sh`
  - `zip-check.sh`: reports whether a zip is already unpacked.
  - `find-dupes.sh`: reports identical files.
- The procedure hands one dataroom to one subagent. The subagent checks each file, removes the original only after the check passes, and writes a `_slim-log.md`.

## Why

Humain's memopop workspace is 3.3GB and has to fit under Cloudflare Artifacts' 1GB per repo to become humain-fs. Most of it is unpacked zips, originals left behind by an earlier compression pass, duplicates, and PDFs full of photos.

Measured on that dataroom: a 162MB PDF went to 23MB, a 23MB research paper to 0.7MB and a scanned certificate from 22.9MB to 10.4MB. Text checks matched within 0.3%, and pages stayed sharp at retina size.

Scripts are one per kind on purpose. A script that converts a whole tree is a fiasco to unpack when one file goes wrong.
