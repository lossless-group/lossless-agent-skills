# Speaker Research Searches for Video on Purpose

## Why Care?

A speaker's past talks are the fastest way to learn how they think and the best
thing to link in a post about them. On the NAESCO R3 agenda, the skill found
none, for anyone: a general search for a name returns LinkedIn and press, and
the skill never said to search for video separately. The "if time is short"
pass also listed everything but video, so video was always the step dropped.

## What changed

- **Step 5 is its own search, every time:** `"<Name>" site:youtube.com` (plus
  the organization for common names), then name plus session topic and talk,
  panel, webinar or keynote, then the organization's and the event's channels.
- **"No video found" needs receipts:** only after those searches ran, with the
  queries written in `not_found`.
- **The fast pass keeps video**, and a new rule covers a whole agenda: a
  baseline for everyone, full research (video included) for the ones that
  matter.
- **Step 2 captures the organization's website** as an `organization_socials`
  entry, because event-hog drafts now link the organizations they name.
- **The example config** tells Firecrawl users to run a separate
  `site:youtube.com` search for video.

The event-hog kit carries a copy of this skill; its server also nudges an agent
that saves a profile with no videos and no record of a video search.
